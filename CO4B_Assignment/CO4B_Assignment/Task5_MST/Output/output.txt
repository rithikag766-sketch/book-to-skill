########## Boruvka tie-break defect (why ties need a consistent rule) ##########
  Triangle, all weights 1. Reasoning (hand-worked, not program output): if X picks X-Y, Y picks Y-Z and
  Z picks Z-X in the same round, all three edges are accepted and form a cycle.
  What this implementation does:
   without tie-break rule (relies on the union-find cycle guard): ['X-Y', 'Y-Z'], cost 2
   with (weight, edge-index) tie-break rule                     : ['X-Y', 'Y-Z'], cost 2
  The union-find guard is what stops the cycle; the consistent tie-break is what makes the choice
  deterministic and is required for the correctness proof when edges are accepted in parallel.
